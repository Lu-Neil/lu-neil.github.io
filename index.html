About me

I am a PhD student at the Centre for Gravitational Astrophysics (CGA) at the Australian National University working on gravitational waves data analysis. I analyse signals from binary black hole mergers to test general relativity and probe black hole physics.

My research interests include black hole spectroscopy and ringdown, tests of gravity, searches for gravitational waves from primordial black hole binaries.

I am a member of the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA collaboration and have contributed to collaboration papers through data analysis and paper writing. Outside research, I am passionate about science outreach, inclusion, and mental health in academic spaces.
